Questions people ask first

How fast can you deliver? ＋

Simple websites target about 3 days once your content, payment and required information are ready. That is a target, not a promise for complex projects. Your quote states the timeline.

Is the price final? ＋

Your quote is fixed for the scope you approve. Anything outside it is priced and agreed before we do it.

What do I pay and when? ＋

50% to begin and 50% on delivery, or the full amount upfront. The arrangement is written on your proposal.

Do I own my website and accounts? ＋

Where practical, your domain, payment gateway and analytics are in your own accounts, and we hand over access at delivery.

What if I want changes? ＋

You get consolidated revision rounds stated in the package. A bug caused by us is fixed and never counts as a revision. New features and pages are quoted separately.

What is not included? ＋

Each package lists exclusions. Domain registration, payment gateway fees, paid APIs, AI usage, premium tools and infrastructure upgrades are disclosed separately.

Can you guarantee rankings or a perfect speed score? ＋

No. We provide basic SEO and practical performance work where scoped, but we do not guarantee rankings or perfect scores.

Do you offer WhatsApp bots? ＋

WhatsApp AI or API bots are not offered. Telegram bot services and website embedded AI assistants are available.

Who pays payment gateway fees? ＋

Your payment gateway account is yours. You complete provider onboarding and pay the provider’s transaction fees.

Can I add more products after launch? ＋

Yes. The store dashboard lets you add products yourself. Product additions after launch are not charged by us as upload work.

What happens if my project changes? ＋

We name the change, quote it and get your approval before doing the extra work.

How long is my price summary valid? ＋

The source brief specifies 7 days. Your written quote remains valid for 7 days from issue.
