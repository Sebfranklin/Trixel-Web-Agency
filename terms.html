Project scope and payment

The signed proposal should define deliverables, exclusions, timeline, revision rounds, payment milestones and acceptance. The website content describes a typical option of 50% to begin and 50% on delivery, or full payment upfront. The agreed proposal controls the actual project.

Changes and revisions

Requests within the agreed revision rounds are handled as consolidated feedback. New pages, features, integrations or other out-of-scope work require a separate quote and approval before work begins.

Client materials and timing

The client is responsible for providing accurate business information, required content, access and timely feedback. Missing materials or delayed approvals may change the delivery timeline.

Third-party costs and ownership

Domain registration, gateway fees, paid APIs, AI usage, premium tools and infrastructure upgrades are third-party costs and should be disclosed in the proposal. Where practical, accounts are created in the client’s own name and access is handed over at delivery.

Support and limitations

Any post-launch support period and its scope must be specified in the proposal. No unlimited revisions, maintenance or hosting is implied. Search rankings and perfect performance scores are not guaranteed.

Before launch, have a qualified professional review this page and align it with your contract, cancellation/refund policy, data handling and applicable Nigerian law.
