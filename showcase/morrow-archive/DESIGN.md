## Overview

EDITORIAL / FASHION MAGAZINE. This site must feel unlike a conventional SaaS landing page. Its personality is editorial / fashion magazine, with a distinct typographic voice and composition.

## Colors

- Background: #f1eee7
- Accent: #b43728
- Contrast surface: #27251f

## Typography

- Display: Bodoni Moda. Reserve for oversized, high-character headlines.
- Body: Arial. Keep body text readable and hierarchy deliberate.

## Layout

Use the site's own composition, not a generic centered hero plus three-card grid. Use full-bleed imagery, irregular grids, and/or asymmetry according to this concept.

## Components

Navigation, buttons, section dividers, and cards must follow this project's unique visual language. Mobile navigation may change form, but do not automatically add a generic hamburger menu.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve the distinct visual system across all sections.
- Do design for keyboard, touch, reduced motion, and readable contrast.
- Don't copy another brand's exact layout or assets.
- Don't reuse the typography, palette, navbar, or card style from another demo.
- Don't use generic gradient blobs or pill buttons unless they are intentional to this brand.

## Multi-page extension
Routes: shop.html, journal.html, about.html. Keep this visual language distinct from other studio projects.
